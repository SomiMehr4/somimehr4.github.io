Dr Somayeh
Mehrabankar

Independent Researcher, Melbourne, Australia
Senior Science Tutor, Fraser's Medical Institute

I am a quantum physicist with a PhD in quantum information theory and over 10 years of international research experience across Iran, Czech Republic, Spain, and Australia. My research focuses on quantum correlations, entanglement theory, open quantum systems, Gaussian and non-Gaussian states of light, and linear optical quantum computing.

I have 11 peer-reviewed publications in leading physics journals and two preprints currently under review at Physical Review A and Physical Review E. I am passionate about both fundamental quantum physics and its translation into real-world quantum technologies.

Research Interests

Quantum Correlations

Entanglement, quantum discord, and quantum steering in bipartite and multipartite systems

Open Quantum Systems

Lindblad master equation, Markovian dynamics, decoherence in coupled oscillator systems

Gaussian & Non-Gaussian States

Quadrature Coherence Scale, non-Gaussianity measures, squeezed thermal states

Quantum Communication

Secure quantum teleportation, quantum key distribution, quantum steering protocols

Linear Optical QC

Heralded entangled state generation, Riemannian optimisation on unitary manifolds

Quantum Simulation

Many-body Hamiltonians, spin ladder systems, qubit reduction techniques
